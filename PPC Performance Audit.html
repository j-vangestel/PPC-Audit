What PPC Performance Audit Does

Complete Account Overview

KPI summary, Campaign Type, and Match Type breakdowns in one view — ACOS and TACOS tracked against your goals, colour-coded so problems jump out immediately.

ASIN-Level Performance

Every ASIN broken out by Child or Parent Rollup, with Organic Sales %, traffic share, and a one-click campaign drill-down — see exactly which campaigns are driving results for any product.

Keyword & Search Term Health

Every keyword and search term automatically classified — Wasted Spend, High ACOS, Winner, Monitor, Underbidding — so you know where to act first, not just what happened.

Placement Performance

Campaign-level Top of Search, Rest of Search, and Product Pages breakdown, with automatic flags for placements worth scaling and spend that's bleeding out.

Year-over-Year Tracking

Add a MerchantSpring export to see Revenue and Units YoY at the account and ASIN level, with automatic New / Up / Down status per product.

Built-In Data Quality Checks

Cross-source validation catches revenue mismatches between your reports before they end up in a client deliverable, not after.

Runs Entirely in Your Browser

No server, no upload beyond your own machine — your account data never leaves your computer.
